About

I'am Aidoko Emmanuel

Web designer

Hi, I'm Emmanuel Simon. A passionate and versatile Software Engineer,Devops, Web Developer, Data Analyst, Graphics Designer, and Animator. I specialize in building smart, efficient, and visually engaging digital solutions that merge creativity with functionality. With a strong foundation in Python (Flask/Django, Pygame, P4Android), HTML/CSS, and MySQL, I create powerful web applications and data-driven tools. I also bring stories to life through animations and graphic design, with a focus on climate change education, tech innovation, and youth empowerment. I'm the founder of Aidoko and Friends Environmental Foundation, where we use comics, animation, and storytelling to raise awareness on environmental issues among children and young adults across Nigeria — in Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, and Igbo. I hold certifications in Software Engineering (ALX), Frontend Development, AI Essentials, Web Development, and Blockchain Technology. I believe in the power of tech for good and constantly explore new ways to use digital tools for real-world impact. Let’s connect — whether it’s to collaborate, create, or make something meaningful.
